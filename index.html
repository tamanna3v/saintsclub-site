আমাদের কথা · About

Saints Club সম্পর্কে

সেইন্টস ক্লাব লিমিটেড (পূর্বনাম গ্রেগরিয়ান ক্লাব) ঢাকার গুলশানে অবস্থিত একটি সামাজিক ক্লাব, যা সেন্ট গ্রেগরি'স হাই স্কুল ও অন্যান্য মিশনারি স্কুলের প্রাক্তন শিক্ষার্থীদের জন্য। গ্রেগরিয়ান অ্যালামনাই ক্লাব নামে এর যাত্রা শুরু হয়; অধ্যাপক জামিলুর রেজা চৌধুরী ক্লাবটির আনুষ্ঠানিক উদ্বোধন করেন এবং শুরুতেই দেশ-বিদেশের ২০০-এর বেশি গ্রেগরিয়ান এর সদস্য হন। ক্লাবের লক্ষ্য বিভিন্ন প্রজন্মের প্রাক্তন শিক্ষার্থীদের মধ্যে সৌহার্দ্য গড়ে তোলা এবং সমাজসেবায় অবদান রাখা।

Saints Club Limited, formerly known as the Gregorian Club, is a social club in Gulshan, Dhaka, for alumni of St Gregory's High School and of other missionary schools. It began as the Gregorian Alumni Club, a new social club started by a group of alumni of St Gregory's High School, the century-old Catholic school in Old Dhaka.

According to a Daily Star report on its launch, the club was formally inaugurated in the Gulshan area by distinguished former student Prof Jamilur Reza Choudhury, who hoisted the club's flag and served as its chief adviser, with Shahidullah Azim as president. At launch the club had already accepted more than 200 Gregorians as members, both from Bangladesh and abroad. Choudhury said the club aimed to build fellowship among alumni across generations and with alumni of other well-known academic institutions, and to give members a way to serve society.

The club premises have also hosted wider alumni activity: the Gregorian Society, a legally registered alumni body of St Gregory's High School and College, held its first executive council meeting at the Gregorian Alumni Club premises when it became operational in 2022.

Background on the parent institution: St Gregory's High School and College traces its origins to 1882, when Benedictine Father Gregory de Groote founded its nucleus. The school moved to its present site in Luxmibazar, Old Dhaka, in 1896, became a boys-only school in 1912, and is run in the tradition of the Congregation of Holy Cross. Its alumni include economist Amartya Sen, Bangladesh's first prime minister Tajuddin Ahmad, and jurist Kamal Hossain.

St. Gregory's High School and College

ওয়েবসাইট ↗
  • Nucleus founded in 1882 by Benedictine Father Gregory de Groote
  • Moved to its present site in Luxmibazar, Old Dhaka, in 1896
  • Became a boys-only school in 1912, when St. Francis Xavier Girls' School opened
  • Granted permanent recognition on 2 June 1924
  • Associated with the Congregation of Holy Cross, which took over the Vicariate of East Bengal from the Benedictines
  • Notable alumni include Amartya Sen, Tajuddin Ahmad and Kamal Hossain
